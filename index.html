Our story

A secret worth
sharing.

Rahsia means secret in Malay — and that's exactly how most people find us: by word of mouth, down a quiet lane in Taman Canning.

Every drink on our menu carries a Malay name that tells you its character. Debu for charcoal-dark, Madu for honey-sweet, Jingga for that caramel orange glow. Pull up a seat, stay a while.

Kopi with character

Eleven coffees, hot or cold — from a straight Legam to a charcoal Debu.

Comfort on a plate

Croffles, French toast, all-day breakfast and oven-baked lasagna.

Small & tucked away

A cosy hideout for catch-ups, quiet work or an afternoon to yourself.
